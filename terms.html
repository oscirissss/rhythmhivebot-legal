What the bot is

RhythmHiveBot is a free, unofficial Discord bot that shows the Rhythm Hive profile and cards of players who link their game account with /link. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by HYBE, Superb, the makers of Rhythm Hive, or Discord.

Using the bot

  • Follow Discord's Terms of Service and Community Guidelines.
  • Link only a Rhythm Hive account that is yours. Never put a code someone else gives you in your profile.
  • Do not use the bot to harass anyone, do not spam its commands, and do not try to break it, overload it or get around its limits.
  • Do not try to use the bot to reach data of accounts that are not linked, or to reach the game's services in ways the bot does not offer.

Your linked account

When you link your account, you agree that its public game data (nickname, level, profile message, cards, scores and ranking) can be shown as pictures in the servers where you and the bot are, by any member who uses /profile or /collection. You can unlink at any time with /link and the remove option. The Privacy Policy explains what is stored and for how long.

The bot never asks for your game password and never logs into your account.
