RhythmHiveBot

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

Español: Política de privacidad

RhythmHiveBot is an independent Discord bot that shows the Rhythm Hive profile and cards of players who choose to link their game account. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by HYBE, Superb, the makers of Rhythm Hive, or Discord.

This page explains what the bot stores, what it only reads to answer you, and how to delete your data.

What the bot stores

  • Your account link. When you finish /link, the bot saves your Discord user ID and the internal ID of the Rhythm Hive account you proved is yours. It stays until you unlink it.
  • A link in progress. While you link, the bot saves your Discord user ID, the nickname you typed and a one-time code. This is deleted when you finish or cancel, and in any case 30 minutes after the code was created.
  • Your language. If you use /language, the bot saves your Discord user ID and the language you chose. Choosing Automatic deletes it.

That is everything the bot keeps about people.

What the bot reads but does not keep

  • From Discord: your user ID, display name and the language of your Discord app, and the server and channel where you use a command. The bot uses them only to answer that command. It cannot read the messages you write.
  • From Rhythm Hive: the public profile of linked accounts (nickname, level, profile message, cards, scores, ranking position and last login date), and the search results for the nickname you type in /link. This data stays only in the bot's memory: about 5 minutes, so the game is not asked again for every click, or while the message that shows it still has its buttons (up to about 15 minutes). It is never written to disk.
  • Pictures: the bot draws the profile and card pictures it posts and keeps recent ones in memory to send them faster. Card art, emblems and profile backgrounds downloaded from the game are kept on disk; they are game images, not personal data. The profile picture shows the background the player equipped in the game.

What is public

/profile and /collection post pictures in the channel where they are used. Anyone who can see that channel can see them, and they stay in Discord until someone deletes the message. In a server where you and the bot are, any member can show the profile and cards of your linked account. Accounts that are not linked cannot be shown.

Who else handles data

The bot does not sell or share your data, does not show ads and does not track you.

  • Rhythm Hive receives the nickname you type in /link (to find the account) and the IDs of the linked accounts whose profiles are shown. The bot never asks for your game password and never logs into your account.
  • Discord delivers the commands and stores the messages the bot posts, under Discord's privacy policy.
  • Railway hosts the bot (servers in Singapore). Its technical error logs may include Discord or game IDs and are kept for a limited time.

Your choices

  • Unlink your account at any time with /link and the remove option. The link is deleted at once.
  • Delete your language choice with /language and Automatic.
  • To ask a question about your data, or to ask for anything else to be deleted, write to the developer (see Contact).

Age

Discord requires users to be at least 13, or the minimum age in their country. The bot collects nothing beyond what this page describes from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page will show the new version and its date.
